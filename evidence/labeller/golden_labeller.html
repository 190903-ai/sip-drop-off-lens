Words you'll see in the posts
SIP
A fixed amount invested automatically every month into a mutual fund.
Mutual fund (MF)
A pooled investment run by a company; you own "units" of it.
Instalment
One monthly payment of the SIP.
Mandate / autopay / e-NACH
Permission for the app to take the money from your bank each month. When it fails, the payment "bounces".
Bounce charge
A fee the bank charges when an automatic payment fails.
Redeem / withdraw
Sell the units and take the money out.
NAV
The price of one unit of the fund on a given day.
Returns / XIRR
How much the investment has grown. XIRR is the correct yearly growth rate for monthly investing.
Small cap / mid cap
Funds in smaller companies: they can grow more but swing up and down a lot.
Direct vs regular plan
The same fund in two versions; "regular" pays a commission, so it costs more.
Lump sum
One big one-time investment instead of monthly.
ELSS
A fund that saves income tax; people stop it when it no longer saves them tax.
FD / RD
Bank fixed or recurring deposits: safe, fixed interest.
Step-up
Raising the monthly SIP amount.
