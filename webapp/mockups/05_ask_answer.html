AnswerFrom 102 first-time stories · 3 citedGemma 4 · checked

In first-time investors' stories, the most common reason is portfolio clean-up (26%): they stop one fund to fix their mix, often while still investing in others1. The most common trigger is their own returns going flat or negative (18%)2.

App problems also appear: trust erosion (15%) and failed payments or mandates (11%), where an instalment fails even though money is in the account3.

How sure is this? 102 stories, mostly Reddit. Quiet drop-offs rarely post, so engaged investors are over-represented. Treat as a hypothesis for interviews.
